How well is it working?

Based on the attempts recorded on this computer.

  • People who were not you were turned away. When someone tried to sign in as you without your face or your typing, the system said no.
  • A photo of you is not enough. Even with a picture of your face, the typing check still turns someone away — and the other way round too.
  • Your typing rhythm is part of your identity. The way you press each key is as identifying as your face.
