Three simple tips

These help ARGUS recognise you reliably without any passwords.

  • Face the camera Keep your head comfortably upright and centred inside the oval guide.
  • Good light on your face A gentle lamp or natural window light in front of you works best.
  • Try a small smile, then a neutral face Natural expressions make daily sign-in quick and easy.
