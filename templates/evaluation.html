Measuring the system

How well is it working?

To know whether ARGUS is any good we need sign-in attempts that are honestly labelled — who was really at the keyboard. Then we can measure how often the wrong person gets in, and how often the right one is turned away.

Step 1 — record some attempts

Have people sign in, and record who each attempt really was.

Record an attempt

Two kinds of attempt, and both matter:

  • The real person. They sign in as themselves, at least 10 times each. This shows how often the system wrongly turns them away.
  • Somebody else. They try to sign in as every other person, at least 5 times each — they already know the passphrase, so only the face and the typing can stop them. This shows how often the system wrongly lets someone in.
